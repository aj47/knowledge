# TechFriendBot Pitch — v1
Date: 2026-02-20

## Target Audience
Ambitious builders, creators, and professionals who want AI deeply integrated into their life and workflow. People who want to 100x their potential with AI.

## Core Pitch (AJ's voice)

Most people use AI like a calculator. You open it, type a question, get an answer, close it. TechFriendBot is different. It's always there. You hold a button, speak, and your AI friend just handles it — browses the web, writes code, posts to Twitter, manages your files, talks to any tool you connect. It's not a chatbot. It's a second brain that actually does things.

We built it for people who think 100x is possible but are still bottlenecked by how slow humans are at context switching. TechFriendBot removes that bottleneck. One voice command can spin up multiple AI agents working in parallel, each a specialist, reporting back to you.

This is what it feels like to have a team, even if you're building solo.

## Key Differentiators
- Voice-first (hold to record, works system-wide)
- Real tool execution via MCP (not just suggestions)
- Multi-agent: spawns parallel specialist sub-agents
- Extensible with Skills (open standard)
- Always-on desktop overlay, not a tab you switch to
- Works with any AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Gemini)
- Mobile app too — agents on the go
