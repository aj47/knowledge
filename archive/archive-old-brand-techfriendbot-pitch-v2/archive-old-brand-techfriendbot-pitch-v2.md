# TechFriendBot — Value Prop Lines
Date: 2026-02-21

## Core Value Lines

- Talk to your computer. It does the work.
- One button. Any task. No typing.
- Your AI agent that actually executes, not just answers.
- OpenClaw needs a server. TechFriendBot needs a download.
- OpenClaw is for hackers. TechFriendBot is for operators.
- Voice in, results out.
- The agent assistant that doesn't require a computer science degree.
- You think it. You say it. It's done.
- Not a chatbot. An agent that lives on your desktop.
- Runs tools, not just text.
- Hold a button. Your agent handles the rest.
- The difference between asking AI and deploying AI.
- OpenClaw deleted a guy's project. TechFriendBot asks first.
- Stop context switching. Start talking.
- Every tool you use, one voice command away.
- Your second brain with hands.
- Spawn 5 agents in parallel. Get back a report.
- Built for people who want 100x output, not 10% faster typing.
