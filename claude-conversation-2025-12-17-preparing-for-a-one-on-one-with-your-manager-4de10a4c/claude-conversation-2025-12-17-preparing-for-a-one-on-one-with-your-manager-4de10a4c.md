**Before the meeting:**

Come with 2-3 specific topics you want to discuss rather than waiting for your manager to drive the agenda. Think about what you need from them—whether that's feedback, clarity on priorities, resources, or career guidance.

Jot down any wins or progress since your last meeting. It's easy to forget accomplishments, and your manager may not have visibility into everything you've done.

If there's something difficult to discuss (a blocker, a concern, a mistake), prepare how you'll frame it. Going in with a clear, non-defensive way to present it helps.

**During the meeting:**

Ask for specific feedback rather than general "how am I doing?" Try something like "What's one thing I could do differently on [specific project]?" or "Where do you see gaps in my work?"

If priorities feel unclear, ask directly: "If I can only get two things done this week, what matters most?"

Take brief notes—especially on action items or commitments from either side.

**Good questions to have ready:**

- What should I be focusing on that I might be missing?
- Is there anything you'd like me to handle differently?
- What's coming up that I should be aware of?
