## Fitness Plan (from Claude conversations, Dec 2025)
> Based on Bryan Johnson's Blueprint Protocol + Peter Attia's Longevity Framework
> Goal: 2.5 hrs/week | Longevity, posture correction, tennis prehab

### Weekly Structure: 5 days/week

#### 🏋️ Mon / Wed / Fri — Gym Days (~40-45 min each)

**1. Posture Correction Block (5 min)**
- Dead hangs — 2 x 30 sec (forward head posture fix + grip strength)
- Hip flexor stretch — 1 min/side (anterior pelvic tilt fix)
- Glute bridges — 2 x 12 reps (APT correction)
- Chin tucks / wall angels — 1 min

**2. Strength — 2:1 Pull-to-Push Ratio (20 min)**
- Cable rows or dumbbell rows — 3 x 10
- Pull-ups or lat pulldown — 3 x 8
- Goblet squats or barbell squats — 3 x 10
- Push-ups or dumbbell bench — 2 x 10

**3. Tennis Joint Prehab (5 min)**
- Banded external rotations — 2 x 12 (shoulders)
- Wrist curls — 2 x 15 (elbows)
- Calf raises — 2 x 15 (ankles)
- Terminal knee extensions — 2 x 12 (knees)

**4. Zone 2 Cycling Cooldown (12-15 min)**
- Stationary bike at 70-80% max HR — conversational pace

#### 🏃 Tue / Thu — Home Sprint Days (~10 min each)
- Quick warm-up (2 min)
- **Skater jumps** — 3-5 all-out sprints, 20-30 sec each, full recovery between
- Mimics tennis lateral movement + builds ankle/knee stability
- This is Zone 5 / VO2 max training

### Key Science (Bryan Johnson / Peter Attia)
- **VO2 Max** = single strongest mortality predictor
- **Grip strength** = 16% lower all-cause mortality per 5kg increase; muscle quality > quantity
- **Zone 2 cardio** = foundation of longevity (mitochondrial health)
- **Exercise sweet spot** = 2.5-5 hrs/week; >10 hrs/week may reduce benefits
- **Four Pillars**: Stability, Strength, Zone 2 (Aerobic), Zone 5 (Anaerobic)

### Posture Targets
- **Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT)**: hip flexor stretches, glute bridges, core work
- **Forward Head Posture**: chin tucks, wall angels, dead hangs, pull emphasis

### Quick 30-Min Version (when short on time)
1. Posture warm-up (3 min) — chin tucks, wall angels, hip flexor stretch
2. Pull-ups or lat pulldown — 3 x 8
3. Cable/dumbbell rows — 3 x 10

5. Dead hangs — 2 x 30 sec
6. Copenhagen plank — 2 x 20 sec/side
7. Regular plank — 2 x 45 sec
8. Zone 2 bike — remaining time (~8-10 min)

## Gym Routine Update (March 2026)

### Why this update
- Keep existing longevity/posture framework, but add a clearer strength structure.
- Prioritize overhead press progress while keeping shoulders healthy.

### New Weekly Structure (recommended)
- **Day 1:** Overhead press + shoulders
- **Day 2:** Pull + legs
- **Day 3:** Rest or light cardio / mobility
- **Day 4:** Push (chest/triceps) + light shoulders
- **Day 5:** Pull + legs variation
- Weekend: 1 full rest day + 1 optional light day

### Day 1 details (Overhead press focus)
- Warm-up sets start very light (e.g. 5 lb dumbbells) for 1–2 sets of 12–15 reps.
- Then ramp up in small jumps to a working weight you can do for 6–10 clean reps with ~1–2 reps in reserve.
- Standing overhead press form:
  - Start in front-rack: weight around upper chest/collarbone, not far in front.
  - Hands just outside shoulder width; wrists stacked over elbows.
  - Press path should be mostly vertical over **mid-foot**.
  - Cue: “chin back → press up → head through at top.”
  - At lockout: biceps near ears, ribs down, glutes + abs braced (no over-arching).
  - Feet: hip- to shoulder-width, toes slightly out, full-foot tripod pressure.

### Best accessory during overhead press rests
- **Top pick:** Face pulls (12–20 reps, controlled)
- Other good options: band pull-aparts (15–25), rear delt flyes (12–20), dead bug/plank (20–40 sec)
- Avoid fatiguing heavy triceps/front raises between press sets.

### Day 2 template (Pull + legs)
- Goblet squat or leg press — 3 x 8–12
- Romanian deadlift — 3 x 8–10
- Lat pulldown / assisted pull-up — 3 x 8–12
- Seated cable row / chest-supported row — 3 x 10–12
- Optional walking lunge — 2 x 10/leg
- Core finisher (plank/dead bug) — 2–3 sets
- Effort target: ~RPE 7–8 (leave 2–3 reps in reserve)

### Integration rule (keep both plans)
- Existing plan remains the health/prehab base (posture, prehab, Zone 2/Zone 5).
- New split becomes the main lifting structure for better progression.
- Keep pull-to-push balance and shoulder-prehab habits as non-negotiables.
