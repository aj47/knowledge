## Bacteriophage (Phage) Therapy — The Leading Approach

Bacteriophages are viruses that exclusively infect and replicate within specific bacterial hosts, making them attractive tools for precise bacterial control in the gut. Unlike antibiotics that carpet-bomb your entire microbiome, phages act like guided missiles.

**How it works:**
- Oral phage therapy targets harmful bacteria with precision while leaving beneficial gut flora intact. You take phage cocktails orally (typically with antacids to survive stomach acid), and they selectively find and destroy only the targeted bacterial strains.
- Clinically, oral phage cocktails are administered at doses of 10⁹ to 10¹⁰ PFU over several days.

**Real clinical results:**
- Personalized phage therapy combined with antibiotics has shown efficacy in eradicating targeted bacteria in 61.3% of cases across 100 patients from 12 countries. Eradication was 70% less likely when antibiotics weren't used alongside the phages.
- Scientists designed a 5-phage cocktail that precisely targets *Klebsiella pneumoniae* strains enriched in IBD patients' guts, and a phase I trial showed the phages survived throughout the GI tract while not impacting surrounding microbiota.

**Cutting-edge approaches:**
- CRISPR-Cas systems are being delivered via engineered phages to cause targeted chromosomal deletions in specific bacteria, effectively programming the phage to edit and disable pathogenic bacteria's genomes.
- A new "Gut Phage Biobank" with 104 phage isolates targeting abundant or disease-associated gut bacteria was recently published, creating a library for future therapeutic use.

**Key challenges:**
- Eliminating one species can cause cascading effects on others — for example, phages targeting *C. sporogenes* also reduced tryptamine levels (a neurotransmitter), even though overall bacterial load remained stable.
- Bacteria can develop resistance through mutations in phage receptors, and the harsh GI environment (stomach acid, bile) can degrade phages before they reach their targets.
- Personalized phage therapy requires specialized centers, and predefined cocktails have sometimes proven ineffective against specific strains, suggesting customization is essential.

**Where things are heading:** The vision is a precision medicine pipeline where clinicians characterize the pathogenic bacteria in a patient's gut, then apply a tailored phage therapy to suppress just those specific organisms.
