## Core Principles That Apply to techfren

**1. Metrics That Matter**
MrBeast reduces YouTube success to three things: CTR (getting clicks), AVD (how long people watch), and AVP (what percentage they complete). He shows data proving that videos with 1.5 minutes longer average watch time get *triple* the views.

**2. The First 60 Seconds Are Everything**
On one video, 21 million viewers left in the first minute. MrBeast's team obsesses over this window—matching thumbnail expectations immediately, pre-planning every word, and front-loading the most interesting content.

**3. Video Structure Blueprint**
- **First minute:** Hook + match expectations
- **Minutes 1-3:** Stop telling, start showing. Show rapid progression.
- **3-minute mark:** Re-engagement "spectacle"—something only you can do
- **Minutes 3-6:** Deepen investment with quick cuts and stimulating content
- **Back half:** Once they're 6 minutes in, they're "in a lull"—less intensive content is acceptable

**4. The "Wow Factor"**
Beyond metrics, MrBeast emphasizes doing things no one else can do. For you, that's your insider perspective at Augment Code, your open-source credibility, and your real-time building.

**5. Title/Thumbnail Rules**
- More extreme language performs better
- "I Survived" beats "I Spent"
- Whatever you promise in the thumbnail, deliver in the first 30 seconds
- Mismatched expectations = instant drop-off

**6. Format Rotation**
"People don't know what they want." Don't do the same format repeatedly—test new approaches, never go back-to-back with the same format.

**7. Information Diet**
You can't get inspired by things you don't know exist. Curate what you consume to fuel creativity.
