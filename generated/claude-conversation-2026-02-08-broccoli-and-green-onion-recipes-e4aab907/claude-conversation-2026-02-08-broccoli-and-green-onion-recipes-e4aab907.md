**Broccoli Green Onion Fritters**
Chop broccoli fine (or pulse in food processor), mix with sliced green onions, 2 eggs, ¼ cup flour, salt, and pepper. Pan-fry spoonfuls in oil until golden on each side, about 3 min per side. Great with sour cream or sriracha mayo.

**Roasted Broccoli with Green Onion Sauce**
Roast broccoli at 425°F with olive oil, salt, and pepper for 20 min. While it roasts, blend green onions with yogurt (or sour cream), lemon juice, garlic, and salt for a creamy sauce. Drizzle over the roasted broccoli.

**Broccoli & Green Onion Fried Rice**
Sauté chopped broccoli in oil, push to the side, scramble eggs in the same pan. Add day-old rice, soy sauce, sliced green onions, and toss everything together. Finish with sesame oil.

## assistant\n\n**Broccoli & Green Onion Rice Noodle Stir-Fry**

**Ingredients**
- 8 oz rice noodles
- 2 cups broccoli florets (cut small)
- 3-4 green onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or more soy sauce)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- Oil for cooking
- Optional: chili flakes, sriracha, or a protein like tofu/shrimp/chicken

**Instructions**

1. Cook rice noodles according to package (usually soak in boiling water 4-6 min). Drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking. Toss with a little oil.

2. Mix the sauce: soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and sugar. Set aside.

3. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Add broccoli and cook 3-4 min until tender-crisp with a little char.

4. Add garlic and green onion whites, stir for 30 seconds.

5. Add noodles and pour the sauce over. Toss everything together for 1-2 min until noodles are coated and heated through.

6. Top with green onion greens and optional chili flakes.

Takes about 15 minutes start to finish. Want any modifications?\n
