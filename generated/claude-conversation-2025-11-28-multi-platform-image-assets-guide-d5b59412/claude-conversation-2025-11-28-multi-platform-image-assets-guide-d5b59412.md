# Image Assets Needed Across Platforms

## 1. Profile Pictures / Avatars
**Used on:** All platforms (YouTube, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Discord)

**Options:**
- **Expressive headshot** – Slight smile, looking at camera, lit with subtle green accent lighting. Conveys approachability + tech credibility.
- **"tf" monogram** – Neon green on black, clean and recognizable at tiny sizes. Works if you want brand-first over personal.
- **Headshot with digital rain overlay** – Your face with subtle Matrix-style characters cascading, blending personal + brand aesthetic.

**Recommendation:** Use the same image everywhere for instant recognition. Headshot with green lighting performs best for trust + follow conversion.
