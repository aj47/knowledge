X Post Strategy Guide

*techfren --- November 2025*

Your Voice: Discovery Energy

Your voice isn\'t \"thought leader with hot takes.\" It\'s **discovery
energy** --- the moment something clicks, the moment something works
that shouldn\'t have. Most tech creators perform excitement. Yours is
real.

Don\'t write thesis statements. Write **discovery reports**.

**The structure:**

-   Here\'s what I discovered/built

-   Here\'s the part that surprised me

-   Here\'s why that matters (or the bigger thing I\'m noticing)

**Example:** *\"Just migrated my app\'s state management to zustand
using subagents. One or two prompts. It cleaned up my entire codebase,
cut 1000 lines of code, and the state management is actually more robust
now. A year ago I tried to build something like this myself. The
frameworks didn\'t even exist. That\'s the gap that keeps hitting me.\"*

How the X Algorithm Works in 2025

The algorithm rewards engagement, relevance, and recency. Understanding
these signals helps your content reach more people.

Key Ranking Signals

-   **Engagement velocity:** The first 5-30 minutes after posting are
    critical. Early engagement signals the algorithm to push your post
    wider.

-   **Replies \> Likes:** Replies carry significantly more weight than
    likes or retweets. Content that sparks conversation wins.

-   **Rich media boost:** Native images and videos get 150% more
    engagement than text-only posts. Video autoplay captures attention.

-   **External links demoted:** Posts with external links are
    deprioritized. Keep people on platform when possible.

-   **Consistency matters:** Active accounts that post regularly get
    algorithmic preference over sporadic posters.

What This Means for You

Post when your audience is active (see timing below). Reply to comments
quickly in that first window. Use images/videos from your builds. Don\'t
just drop links --- give context and value in the post itself.

When to Post

Data from 2025 shows consistent patterns, but your specific audience may
vary. Use these as starting points, then check your analytics.

Best Days

-   **Highest engagement:** Tuesday through Thursday

-   **Lowest engagement:** Saturday and Sunday

Best Times (Your Time Zone)

-   **Morning window:** 8-10 AM (catching people starting their day)

-   **Lunch window:** 12-2 PM (lunch break scrolling)

-   **Evening window:** 6-9 PM (after work, high engagement)

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Section**     **Purpose**                **Time**

**3.1 Script Notes Template System**

Script notes are sentence starters you glance at while recording.
They\'re not full scripts---just prompts to remind you what points to
hit. You finish each sentence naturally on camera, keeping your
authentic voice.

**Why It Works:**

-   Keeps your delivery natural (not memorized/robotic)

-   Ensures you hit all key points without forgetting

-   Easy to glance at while screen recording

-   Preserves your \"discovery energy\" voice

**How to Write Script Notes**

1.  **Start with the outcome.** What does the viewer walk away knowing?
    Write that first, then work backward.

2.  **Write sentence starters, not full sentences.** Each bullet is 3-6
    words that trigger the rest of the thought. You\'ll complete it
    naturally on camera.

3.  **Use ellipsis to signal continuation.** End each starter with
    \"\...\" so your brain knows to keep going.

4.  **Group by section with clear headers.** Bold section headers (HOOK,
    VALUE STACK, DEMO, CLOSE) so you can quickly find your place.

5.  **Keep it to one page.** If you can\'t see everything at once,
    you\'ll lose your place. Trim until it fits.

**Example Format**

**HOOK**

> • \[Result or bold claim\]\...
>
> • \[What this is\]\...

**VALUE STACK**

> • I use \[tool\] and\...
>
> • No \[pain point\]\...

**DEMO**

> • \[First action\]\...
>
> • Let\'s see if\...

**CLOSE**

> • \[Action CTA: try it, download, check it out\]\...

**4.3 The 7 Scripting Rules**

6.  **Lead with a claim, not context.** \"This is trending with 20K
    stars\" beats \"Let me show you this tool.\" Make the viewer curious
    about the claim before explaining it.

7.  **Never start with \"so,\" \"hey,\" or \"what\'s up.\"** These slow
    starts kill retention. Jump directly into value.

8.  **Name the tool in the first 5 seconds.** 40% of your top performers
    do this vs 25% of bottom. Specificity signals value immediately.

9.  **Use comparisons for credibility.** \"better than GPT-4\" appears
    in 18% of top videos vs 8% of bottom. Comparisons create instant
    context.

10. **Cut the filler words.** Your top videos have 14% fewer fillers
    (\"like,\" \"basically,\" \"you know\"). Script tightly.

11. **Don\'t ask---claim.** Question hooks have a 29% success rate. Bold
    claims have 80%. Make statements, not questions.

12. **\"Free\" and \"open source\" are magic words.** 2.5x more top
    performers mention these. They signal immediate value.
