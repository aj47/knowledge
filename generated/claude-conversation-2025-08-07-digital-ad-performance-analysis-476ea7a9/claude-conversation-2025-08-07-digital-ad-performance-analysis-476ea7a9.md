## Key Insights from Digital Advertising Performance

### 📊 Overall Performance Summary

**July Results:**
- **Total spend**: $301,850 (slightly over budget by $1,850)
- **Customer acquisition cost**: $1,670 per customer
- **Conversion funnel**: 5,910 sign-ups → 160 customers (2.7% overall conversion rate)
- **Warning**: Conversion tracking was broken July 17-26, so numbers are likely undercounted

**August Pacing** (as of day 7):
- Currently 107.5% of expected pace, tracking to slightly overspend if maintained

### 🎯 Channel Performance Breakdown

**Winners:**
1. **Google Search** - Dominates with 71% of spend and lowest CAC
   - $45 per sign-up (best efficiency)
   - Strong conversion funnel despite tracking issues

2. **YouTube Remarketing** - High efficiency when targeting right audiences
   - Website visitors: $58 per sign-up (best performer)
   - 5.4x more efficient than prospecting campaigns

**Losers:**
1. **Reddit** - Severe underperformance
   - $730 per sign-up (16x worse than Google)
   - Multiple campaigns being paused
   - Question raised: "How much are we willing to spend on this billboard?"

2. **Twitter** - Budget control issues
   - Spent 349% of allocated budget in July ($3,490 vs $1,000 budget)
   - Only 2 sign-ups despite overspend

### 🚨 Critical Issues Requiring Immediate Attention

1. **Meta Pixel** - Currently not working, impacting Facebook tracking
2. **Google Ads Copyright Infringement** - Awaiting confirmation code
3. **YouTube Windsurf Targeting** - Cannibalizing 77% of impressions with worst performance
4. **Reddit Strategy** - Need decision on whether to continue (currently just brand awareness)

### 💡 Optimization Opportunities

**Quick Wins:**
- **YouTube**: Focus solely on Cursor targeting, pause Windsurf (could save ~$10K/month)
- **Display Ads**: Scale the green "confidence" creative (2.5x better CTR)
- **Remarketing**: Shift more budget to website visitor remarketing

**Budget Reallocation Recommendations:**
- Reduce Reddit from $40K to $10K (or pause entirely)
- Increase Google Search budget by 10-15%
- Shift YouTube budget from prospecting to remarketing
- Set hard caps on Twitter to prevent overspend

### 📈 Conversion Funnel Analysis

The funnel shows significant drop-offs:
- Sign-up → Activated: 31.3% (needs improvement)
- Activated → CC Added: 25.2% (major bottleneck)
- CC Added → Customer: 34.3%

**Recommendation**: Focus on improving the activation and credit card addition experience rather than just driving more top-of-funnel traffic.

### 🎬 Action Items

1. **Immediate**: Fix Meta pixel and resolve Google Ads copyright issue
2. **This week**: Pause underperforming Reddit campaigns and Windsurf targeting
3. **Strategic**: Re-evaluate Reddit's role (brand awareness vs. performance)
4. **Testing**: Scale successful creative formats across channels

The data suggests a need to consolidate spend on proven channels (Google, YouTube remarketing) while fixing technical issues and cutting underperforming campaigns.\n
