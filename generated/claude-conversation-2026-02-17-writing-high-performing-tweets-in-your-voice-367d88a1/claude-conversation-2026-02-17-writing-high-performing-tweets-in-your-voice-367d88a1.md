## assistant\n\nBased on your data and voice guides, here's the essentials:

**Lead with discovery, not commentary.** Your best-performing content follows a "discovery report" structure: what you found → what surprised you → why it matters. You're not a pundit—you're a builder sharing what you just saw work.

**First words are everything.** Bold claims hit 80% success rate vs 29% for questions. Start with the result or the surprising thing, never with "so," "hey," or throat-clearing. Name the tool or specific metric immediately.

**Write like you talk on your best days.** Your power words: "just," "actually," "dude," "pretty cool," "boom," "free," "open source." Your voice is confident and direct—cut "I think," "kind of," "maybe." Assertive beats hedging every time.

**Format for scroll speed.** One idea per line. Whitespace between thoughts. Cut every word that isn't earning its place. 70-100 characters per line for quick-read posts.

**End with action, not asks.** "Download it" and "try it" correlate with 8x more views than "subscribe" or "follow." Give people something to do.

**The vibe:** You're the peer who's slightly ahead, showing what's possible with genuine excitement. Not performing, discovering. Not reviewing, building. Every post should feel like you just came back from the workshop with something cool to show.\n
