## Esports Tournament Formats (Steal These)

**Single Elimination:** One loss = out. High stakes, unforgiving, great drama.

**Double Elimination:** Lose once → drop to lower bracket. Losers can still win. More forgiving, longer event.

**Swiss Format:** Everyone plays same number of rounds, matched by win record. Good for large fields.

**Round Robin → Finals:** Everyone plays everyone, top performers advance to bracket. Best for smaller groups.

## Challenge Complexity Sweet Spot

For 90 minutes, the challenge needs to be:
- **Achievable** by a good vibe coder in 60-75 min (buffer for bugs)

**Too hard:** "Build a full SaaS with auth and payments" (nobody finishes)

**Just right:** "Build a tool that [specific user problem] with [one interesting constraint]"
