# "Shadow work on limiting beliefs and subconscious patterns" (Evergreen Distilled)

## Core takeaways

- Claude acted as a shadow work facilitator, drawing on the user's extensive self-reflection materials including beliefs profiles and pattern analysis.
- The user is 32, has 100k in savings, currently works full-time but plans to pursue entrepreneurship when visa constraints lift in July 2026, and values autonomy above almost everything else.
- The session uncovered and dissolved a key limiting belief: the user's fear of success, which they initially framed as "success means being locked into something" with investors and users owning their time.
- Through exploration, they realized this belief assumed success must be misaligned, when in fact they could build something successful while preserving autonomy by designing alignment from the start.
- When this reframe clicked, the fear dissolved and was replaced by empowerment.

## Reusable insights

- The conversation then examined another belief about needing 1.5 million dollars (25 years of runway at 60k/year) before starting a family to be a fully present stay-at-home father.
- The user recognized this as potentially unrealistic and a moving goalpost that could keep fatherhood perpetually out of reach.
- They identified their core pattern: waiting for certainty before committing, but certainty only comes through commitment.
