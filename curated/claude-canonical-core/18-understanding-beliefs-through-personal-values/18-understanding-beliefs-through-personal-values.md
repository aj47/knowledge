# Understanding beliefs through personal values

## Essence

- Acting as a professional psychologist and philosopher specialized in beliefs, Claude helped the user excavate deep-seated beliefs that have been influencing their life decisions for over a decade.
- The user's core values were identified as autonomy, mastery and creation, focused intimacy over broad connection, authenticity, and freedom through stability.
- Through systematic questioning, the conversation revealed a central belief loop: "I'm waiting for certainty before I commit.

## Operating principles

- Certainty never arrives." This pattern has prevented the user from fully committing to any project for over ten years, despite having software development skills and multiple ideas.
- The exploration uncovered beliefs about worthiness and earning, particularly around family planning.
- They have substantial runway and recognize that their "safe path" of maintaining employment while building side projects is actually the "slow path" that may prevent them from achieving their life goals.
