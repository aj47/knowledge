# Current High-Level Goals

This note captures the **current preferred framing** of AJ's high-level goals.

It should be preferred over older goal framings when the user asks for a more ambitious, directional, less maintenance-oriented version.

## Current preferred goal set

1. **Build one undeniable breakout thing**
   - Not just improve DotAgents incrementally.
   - Create something so sharp, useful, and demoable that people immediately get why it matters.

2. **Become a top-of-mind taste-maker in AI agents**
   - Be known not only for building, but for having strong product taste, clear opinions, and original framing.
   - The goal is cultural and intellectual positioning, not just shipping.

3. **Create a compounding distribution machine**
   - Turn work into repeatable attention.
   - Build a system where every product insight becomes posts, demos, clips, threads, and reputation growth.

4. **Design a life with more leverage, less fragmentation**
   - Reduce the feeling of splitting across too many contexts.
   - Build around fewer, bigger bets with more downstream upside.

5. **Build an operating system that upgrades itself**
   - Not just notes or tasks.
   - A real personal-agent system that improves decision-making, memory, prioritization, and execution over time.

6. **Convert skill into ownership**
   - Use technical ability, brand, and product sense to create assets with long-term upside: product equity, audience, systems, and IP.

## Compressed north star

**Make AJ a breakout AI-agent builder with a flagship product, a real audience, and a self-improving system that compounds.**

## Usage guidance

When summarizing AJ's goals:
- prefer this framing when the request is about current ambition, direction, or higher-level strategy
- use older personal systems notes as supporting context, not the default answer
- avoid drifting back into overly maintenance-oriented or purely balancing language unless explicitly asked
## Even higher-level north star

**Own an important idea about how AI agents should work, embody it in a flagship platform, and use systems + proof + distribution to turn that into lasting influence and ownership.**

## Preference rule

- When the user asks to go *higher level* than the current preferred goal set, prefer the even-higher-level north-star framing above before falling back to the more operational six-goal set.
