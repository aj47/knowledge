# AJ High-Level Goals + Systems
**Created:** 2026-03-13
**Purpose:** Define the higher-level outcomes AJ wants from life and the recurring systems that make those outcomes more likely.

---

## How to use this document
This is not a daily to-do list. It is a compass.

Use it to:
- decide what matters this year
- avoid overcommitting to low-leverage work
- translate identity into recurring weekly behavior
- review whether current effort matches stated priorities

A goal is the destination.
A system is the repeated behavior/environment that gets you there.

---

## Goal 1: Build DotAgents into a category-defining product
### Outcome
Turn DotAgents into a trusted, differentiated AI agent product with real user pull, clear positioning, and steady product quality improvements.

### Why it matters
This is the strongest current vehicle for AJ's product ambition, technical taste, and long-term leverage.

### Success looks like
- clear roadmap and issue prioritization
- regular shipping cadence
- fewer ambiguous PRs / stale branches / unresolved product questions
- stronger user trust through reliability and polish
- increasing public proof: users, stars, demos, references, retention signals

### Core systems
- Weekly roadmap review tied to actual GitHub issue state
- 2 focused product-building blocks per week for highest-leverage issues
- PR hygiene block every week: merge, close, clarify, or rewrite scope
- Keep decision logs for important product tradeoffs
- Publish visible progress artifacts so product momentum is externally legible

### Guardrails
- Don't confuse motion with traction
- Don't let old plans survive unchanged after the repo reality has shifted
- Finish and simplify before expanding surface area

---

## Goal 2: Become known for AI agents + developer-tooling ideas
### Outcome
Build a recognizable personal brand around thoughtful, practical, high-signal work in AI agents, product design, and developer tooling.

### Why it matters
Brand is a moat. Distribution compounds. AJ's ability to explain, demo, and tell stories is underutilized and likely one of the highest-ROI assets available.

### Success looks like
- consistent publishing cadence
- people associate AJ / TechFren with useful opinions and product demos
- content drives discovery for DotAgents and future opportunities
- stronger audience trust, not just occasional spikes

### Core systems
- Ship at least 1 high-signal public artifact per week
- Convert product work into content within 24-72 hours of shipping
- Maintain a running list of post ideas, hooks, screenshots, and lessons
- Reuse one idea across formats: post, thread, short demo, recap, video
- Treat video as a KPI, not a bonus task

### Guardrails
- Don't wait for perfect polish before posting
- Don't let content become disconnected from actual work
- Prefer proof, demos, and lessons over generic commentary

---

## Goal 3: Create a sustainable two-track career system
### Outcome
Operate in a way where Augment work and personal work both benefit from AJ's strengths without causing constant context fragmentation or burnout.

### Why it matters
AJ has meaningful ambition in both worlds. The challenge is not capability but designing a system where each track strengthens rather than cannibalizes the other.

### Success looks like
- clear separation of Augment vs DotAgents priorities
- lower cognitive residue between contexts
- realistic commitments and fewer overloaded weeks
- more energy left for the work that matters most

### Core systems
- Separate planning lanes for Augment and DotAgents
- Weekly capacity check before committing to side-project scope
- Define one concrete win per track each week
- Use explicit shutdown / handoff rituals between work contexts
- Protect creative energy for the most leverage-heavy task, not just the most urgent task

### Guardrails
- Don't let personal ambition quietly spill into impossible weekly scope
- Don't use productive-feeling busywork as a substitute for important work
- Don't collapse both tracks into one giant undifferentiated backlog

---

## Goal 4: Build a personal operating system that compounds
### Outcome
Run life and work through simple, reliable review loops that increase focus, self-trust, and execution quality over time.

### Why it matters
AJ does best when the system reduces drift. The bottleneck is often not intelligence or creativity, but consistency, prioritization, and emotional steadiness.

### Success looks like
- priorities are visible and current
- fewer weeks lost to ambiguity or scattered focus
- better follow-through
- more decisions made from reflection, not mood
- systems become easier to maintain over time

### Core systems
- Weekly review: goals, calendar, obligations, unfinished decisions
- Daily top-1 or top-3 priority check
- Monthly reset: what is working, what is noise, what to remove
- Keep lightweight notes on lessons, blockers, and repeated failure modes
- Use environment design to reduce friction and temptation

### Guardrails
- Don't overbuild the system until it becomes procrastination
- Prefer simple rituals that survive busy weeks
- Reflection only matters if it changes behavior

## Goal 5: Stay physically durable, attractive, and high-energy
### Outcome
Maintain a body that supports confidence, longevity, tennis, posture, and sustained output.

### Why it matters
Health is not separate from ambition. Energy, posture, strength, and joint durability affect mood, consistency, and presence.

### Success looks like
- consistent weekly training
- knee durability improving for tennis
- posture work maintained
- strength progressing gradually
- fewer gaps where exercise disappears entirely

### Core systems
- Weekly minimum training floor that still counts during busy weeks
- Log workout notes immediately after sessions
- Keep tennis-prehab and posture work as defaults, not optional extras
- Progress strength with a simple repeatable structure
- Protect sleep and recovery when workload rises

### Guardrails
- Don't chase intensity at the expense of consistency
- Don't rely on motivation; rely on preset routines
- Health baseline first, optimization second

## Goal 6: Build an intentional adult life outside of work
### Outcome
Create a stable, meaningful life that includes marriage, home, peace of mind, future family readiness, and enjoyment of the present.

### Why it matters
Work goals matter, but they sit inside a bigger life. AJ's long-term goals include family, home ownership, comfortable travel, and worry-free living.

### Success looks like
- strong marriage and quality time with Chloe
- home life feels restorative, not chaotic
- money goals are becoming real, not abstract
- life feels designed rather than improvised
- progress toward house / family / income goals is visible

### Core systems
- Regular relationship time protected in calendar
- Monthly personal-finance review
- Track progress toward income and savings targets
- Keep home organized enough to support calm and creativity
- Revisit long-term life goals quarterly so they influence present decisions

### Guardrails
- Don't let work consume the life it is supposed to fund
- Don't postpone all joy until after success
- Stability is a strategic advantage, not a distraction

## Annual theme
**Make tech cool by building, explaining, and living in a way that compounds.**

This means:
- build real products
- communicate clearly and publicly
- stay healthy enough to sustain the pace
- create systems that reduce drift
- turn effort into reputation, leverage, and long-term freedom

## Weekly scoreboard (simple version)
Use this once per week.

Rate each from 0-2:
- Product: Did I move DotAgents forward in a meaningful way?
- Distribution: Did I publish something high-signal?
- Career balance: Did I protect both Augment and personal priorities?
- Operating system: Did I do my review loops and act on them?
- Health: Did I train and log notes?
- Life: Did I invest in marriage / home / future stability?

Interpretation:
- 0 = neglected
- 1 = partial
- 2 = solid

Max score = 12.
The goal is not perfection. The goal is visibility and course correction.
