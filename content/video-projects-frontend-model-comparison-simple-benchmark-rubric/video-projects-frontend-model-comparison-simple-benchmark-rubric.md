# Simple benchmark grading rubric

Use one overall 1–5 score for each category.

## Scoring scale

**1** = poor / broken / misses the point  
**2** = weak / noticeably flawed  
**3** = okay / usable but generic  
**4** = strong / polished  
**5** = excellent / standout

## Core categories

### 1. Visual quality
Does it look polished, well spaced, and visually appealing?

Look for:
- strong hierarchy
- good spacing
- clean typography
- tasteful color use
- overall polish

### 2. Originality and taste
Does it feel distinctive and intentional, or just like generic AI SaaS UI?

Look for:
- memorable visual identity
- strong creative direction
- restraint
- avoidance of clichés

### 3. Product understanding
Does the design actually understand the Intent product and its concepts?

Look for:
- agent orchestration
- multiple workspaces / worktrees
- spec-driven workflow
- approvals, artifacts, blockers, progress
- believable product structure

### 4. Landing page quality
Is the marketing page clear, compelling, and well designed?

Look for:
- strong hero
- clear messaging
- good section flow
- credibility / CTA
- alignment with the product story

### 5. Dashboard quality
Does the app UI feel useful, believable, and easy to understand?

Look for:
- clear overview
- understandable navigation
- good information density
- actionable states
- feels like a real product, not concept art

### 6. Cohesion
Do the landing page and dashboard feel like the same product system?

Look for:
- shared visual language
- shared product story
- consistent tone and design system

### 7. Responsiveness and implementation
Does it actually work well as a frontend deliverable?

Look for:
- runs correctly
- responsive layouts
- no major visual breakage
- both landing page and dashboard delivered
- reasonable code / implementation quality

## Simple final score

You can either:

### Option A: equal weight
Average all 7 categories.

### Option B: weighted score
- Visual quality: 25%
- Originality and taste: 20%
- Product understanding: 20%
- Landing page quality: 10%
- Dashboard quality: 15%
- Cohesion: 5%
- Responsiveness and implementation: 5%

## Fast on-camera version

If you want an even simpler version for the video, score each model on just 4 things:

1. **Looks good**  
2. **Feels original**  
3. **Understands the product**  
4. **Feels like a real startup frontend**

That gives you a fast, easy-to-explain benchmark without losing the point of the test.
