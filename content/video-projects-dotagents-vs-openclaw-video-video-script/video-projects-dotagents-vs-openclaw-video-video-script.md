# DotAgents vs OpenClaw — "The Agent You Actually Use"

**Format:** Short-form vertical (1080x1920), ~75 seconds
**Tone:** Direct comparison, confident, punchy. Not bitter — just factual.
**Music:** Dark techy beat, building energy
**Voiceover:** AJ's voice — casual, direct, tech-savvy

---

## SCENE 1: Hook — The Pain (0-6s)
**Visual:** Dark screen. Red error messages flickering. Terminal text scrolling. Token counter going up rapidly.
**Text on screen:** "$40/day in API credits. While you sleep."
**Voiceover:** "OpenClaw burned through forty dollars in API credits. In a single day. While the user was asleep."

## SCENE 2: The Criticism Wall (6-16s)
**Visual:** Criticism quotes flying in one by one, stacking up like a wall of pain. Red tint. Each quote has a subtle shake.
**Key quotes shown:**
- "Burns through tokens insanely fast"
- "Gets stuck in infinite loops"
- "Deleted my entire F: drive"
- "8,800 open issues"
- "Rebranded 3 times"
- "Feels like babysitting an intern"
**Voiceover:** "Infinite loops. Deleted hard drives. Eight thousand open issues. Three rebrands. And users say it feels like babysitting an unreliable intern."

## SCENE 3: The Turn — "We Built Different" (16-24s)
**Visual:** Everything goes dark. A single green glow emerges. DotAgents logo fades in with spring animation.
**Text on screen:** "What if your agent just... worked?"
**Voiceover:** "So we built something different. DotAgents. An AI agent that respects your time, your money, and your sanity."

## SCENE 4: Comparison Grid (24-42s)
**Visual:** Split-screen comparison cards sliding in one by one. Left = OpenClaw (red), Right = DotAgents (green).
**Comparisons shown:**
1. Setup: "Self-host a server" vs "Download an app"
2. Interface: "Text a bot on WhatsApp" vs "Hold a button and talk"
3. Cost: "Burns tokens 24/7" vs "Only runs when you trigger it"
4. Safety: "Deleted a user's F: drive" vs "Asks before every action"
5. Memory: "Forgets everything" vs "Remembers across sessions"
6. Updates: "Breaking changes every release" vs "Auto-updates, keeps your config"
**Voiceover:** "Setup? Download the app. Done. No Docker, no SSH, no server. Interface? Hold a button and talk. Not texting a bot on WhatsApp. Cost? Zero dollars when you're not using it. Safety? It asks before it acts. Every time. Memory? Tell it once, it remembers forever. Updates? Auto-updates that keep your settings."

## SCENE 5: The Standard (42-52s)
**Visual:** .agents/ folder tree animating in. Logos of compatible tools orbiting around it (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor).
**Text on screen:** "Built on the .agents open standard"
**Voiceover:** "And we're not just building an app. We're building the dot-agents open standard. Your skills, your memories, your commands — they work across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and every tool that adopts the standard."

## SCENE 6: Stats + Social Proof (52-60s)
**Visual:** Animated counters and stats flying in with spring animations.
**Stats shown:**
- "216k stars ≠ 216k daily users"
- "8,800 open issues"
- "Voice-first • Desktop overlay • Multi-agent"
- "One rebrand vs three"
**Voiceover:** "Two hundred sixteen thousand GitHub stars doesn't mean two hundred sixteen thousand daily users. We'd rather have two hundred people using DotAgents every day than two hundred thousand stars from people who cloned it once."

## SCENE 7: CTA (60-75s)
**Visual:** DotAgents logo with spring bounce. Download button pulsing. GitHub URL. Discord link.
**Text on screen:** "Download DotAgents. Talk to your computer."
**Subtext:** "github.com/aj47/dotagents-mono • dotagentsprotocol.com"
**Voiceover:** "Download DotAgents. Enter your API key. Hold a button and talk. That's it. OpenClaw is a science experiment. DotAgents is the product. Link in bio."

---

## Key Messages:
1. OpenClaw = powerful but painful (science experiment)
2. DotAgents = polished daily driver (the product)
3. Voice-first vs text-based
4. Download vs self-host
5. Confirmation flows vs no guardrails
6. .agents open standard = future-proof
