## human\n\ncan i opt out of vaccines\n
## assistant\n\nShort answer: **not really**, not for the I-693.

The CDC sets the vaccine requirements for immigration, and civil surgeons are required to follow them. Unlike regular healthcare where you can decline, this is a condition of getting your green card.

There are only two exceptions:

**Medical waiver** — if a doctor determines a vaccine is medically contraindicated for you (e.g., allergy, immune condition). The civil surgeon documents this on the I-693.

**Religious/moral objection** — this used to be an option but **USCIS eliminated the blanket religious exemption in 2021**. It's essentially no longer available.

So if you're missing vaccines, you either get them or your I-485 gets denied. It's one of the non-negotiable parts of the process.

The practical move is still the same — get titers for Hep B and varicella first. If you already have immunity from childhood, you don't need those vaccines at all. That could knock out two of the five you're missing, and the remaining ones (MMR dose 2, Tdap booster, flu shot) are all single quick shots you can knock out in one pharmacy visit.\n
