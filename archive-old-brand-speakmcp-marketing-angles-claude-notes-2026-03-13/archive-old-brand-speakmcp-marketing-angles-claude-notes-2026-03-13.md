# SpeakMCP Marketing Angles — Claude Notes Distillation

Source conversation: `AI Agent Voice Delegation Platform`
Date distilled: 2026-03-13
Status: archived historical positioning

## Context
This note comes from the SpeakMCP era and should be treated as historical brand/context material rather than active DotAgents messaging.

## Core claim explored
“Best UX for agent delegation”

## Marketing angles captured
### 1. Accessibility / natural interface
Voice as the most natural delegation surface.
Potential appeal:
- hands-free workflows
- lower friction for non-technical users
- more human-feeling interaction with agent systems

### 2. Fast command and dispatch UX
Delegating to agents via speech can feel faster than tabbing through interfaces, menus, or long text prompts.

### 3. Orchestration as product, not just chat
The idea was not merely talking to one assistant, but routing work across multiple agents in a way that feels immediate.

### 4. Demo-friendly novelty
Voice-controlled delegation is visually and conceptually sticky, which makes it good for marketing, demos, and social clips.

## Target markets considered
- AI power users who want faster command dispatch
- builders experimenting with multi-agent workflows
- accessibility-conscious users
- creators/demo-driven early adopters attracted to novel interfaces

## Risk in the positioning
If positioned only as a novelty voice interface, the product can feel like a gimmick.
The stronger angle is productivity + orchestration + reduced friction.

## Historical note
This note is archived because active work has moved from SpeakMCP to DotAgents.
