- Certain genres of games promote problem-solving, collaboration, creativity, and reaction skills. This can potentially have cognitive and social benefits.

- The video game industry provides jobs and economic growth. The global video game market is worth over $100 billion.

Cons of video games:

- Time spent playing games displaces time that could be spent on more "productive" activities like work, exercise, socializing, etc. 

- Problematic gaming behaviors like addiction are a concern for a minority of gamers.

- Violent games could potentially promote aggression in some individuals, though this remains controversial.

- Games with exploitative monetization practices (loot boxes, etc.) can enable "predatory" business models.

Overall there are reasonable arguments on both sides. There are both benefits and costs to gaming's prominence in society. It's difficult to conclusively say if we'd have "progressed further" without video games given their complex effects. Reasonable people can disagree on this issue.\n
