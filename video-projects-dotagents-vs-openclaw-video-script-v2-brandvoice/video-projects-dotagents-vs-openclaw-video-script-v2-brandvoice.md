# DotAgents vs OpenClaw — Video Script v2 (techfren Brand Voice)
**Total runtime:** ~90 seconds
**Voice:** Discovery energy, bold claims, confident, punchy. No "so/hey/what's up" openers.

---

## Scene 1: HOOK (0–8s)
**Visual:** Token counter racing, dollar amount climbing in red, "While you slept."

**VO:**
"OpenClaw just burned forty dollars in API credits. While you were sleeping. That's not a bug — that's how it works."

**Notes:** Bold claim opener. Specific number. Immediate tension. No preamble.

---

## Scene 2: CRITICISM WALL (8–19s)
**Visual:** Red criticism cards slamming in from both sides, shaking. Red pulse background.

**VO:**
"And it gets worse. It deleted someone's entire F drive. It gets stuck in infinite loops. It has eight thousand eight hundred open issues. It rebranded three times — the old env vars are still in the code. People are literally babysitting this thing twenty-four seven."

**Notes:** Rapid-fire pain points. Stack them fast. Let the visuals reinforce each one.

---

## Scene 3: THE TURN (19–28s)
**Visual:** Everything goes black. Green glow emerges. DotAgents logo scales in. "What if your agent just... worked?"

**VO:**
"I built something different. DotAgents. Download the app. Enter your API key. Hold a button. Talk. That's it."

**Notes:** Contrast moment. Calm confidence after chaos. Use "just" — it signals simplicity.

---

## Scene 4: COMPARISON GRID (28–52s)
**Visual:** Side-by-side cards. Red ✕ OpenClaw vs Green ✓ DotAgents. Rows animate in one by one.

**VO:**
"Setup? OpenClaw needs a server, Docker, SSH. DotAgents is a download.

Interface? OpenClaw — you're texting a bot on WhatsApp. DotAgents — you hold a button and talk.

Cost? OpenClaw burns tokens around the clock. DotAgents only runs when you trigger it. Zero dollars when you're not using it.

Safety? OpenClaw gave someone full shell access with no guardrails. DotAgents asks before every single action. Kill switch built in.

Memory? OpenClaw forgets everything between sessions. DotAgents remembers. You explain once.

Updates? OpenClaw breaks something every release. DotAgents auto-updates and keeps your config."

**Notes:** Punchy pairs. Short sentences. Each comparison lands like a fact, not an opinion. "Just" and "actually" are power words here.

---

## Scene 5: THE STANDARD (52–67s)
**Visual:** .agents/ folder tree typing in. Tools orbiting the .agents logo — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf.

**VO:**
"And here's the thing nobody's talking about. DotAgents isn't just an app — it's built on the dot-agents open standard. Your skills, your memories, your config — they work across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf. Write a skill once, it runs everywhere. OpenClaw locks you into their proprietary ClawHub. We gave you a protocol."

**Notes:** This is the unique discovery moment — "here's the thing nobody's talking about" = curiosity gap. Lean into builder credibility.

---

## Scene 6: STATS (67–83s)
**Visual:** 216k counter in background. Red stat cards for OpenClaw, green for DotAgents.

**VO:**
"Two hundred sixteen thousand GitHub stars. Impressive. But stars don't mean daily users. Stars don't fix eight thousand open issues. Stars don't stop your agent from going rogue at 3 AM.

DotAgents is voice-first. Desktop overlay. Multi-agent delegation with actual guardrails. One rebrand — because we were busy building the standard, not picking a new name."

**Notes:** Acknowledge the competition's strength, then reframe it. Confident, not bitter. "Actually" and "real" are power words.

---

## Scene 7: CTA (83–98s)
**Visual:** DotAgents logo with green glow. Download button pulsing. GitHub and protocol links. Closing line fades in.

**VO:**
"Download DotAgents. Talk to your computer. That's the whole pitch.

No server. No Docker. No two-AM SSH debugging. Your agent asks before it acts. It remembers what you told it. And it costs zero when you're not using it.

OpenClaw is a science experiment. DotAgents is the product. Check it out."

**Notes:** Action CTA — "download" and "check it out" outperform "subscribe" by 8x per the data. End on the one-liner. Boom.
