**Thai Red Curry Chicken**

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
- 1 can coconut milk (400ml)
- 2 tbsp red curry paste

- 1 tsp sugar
- Vegetables (bell peppers, snap peas, spinach, whatever you've got)

**Steps**
