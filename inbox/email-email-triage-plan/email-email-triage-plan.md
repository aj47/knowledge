# Email Triage Plan — AJ's Gmail
**Created:** 2026-02-25

## Overview
Automated repeat task (every 30 min) that triages up to 10 unlabeled inbox emails per run, most recent first.

## AJ's Profile (for triage context)
- Tech creator/builder: @techfren / @techfren_ai brand
- Building DotAgents (formerly SpeakMCP) — open-source voice-first AI agent desktop app
- Works full-time at Augment Code (E3 visa — cannot freelance or take other employment)
- YouTube/TikTok/X content creator focused on making tech cool
- Based in SF/Bay Area, originally from Australia
- Goal: 20k/month income, grow brand, maximize authentic high-value collabs

## Collab Evaluation Criteria (PRIORITY ORDER)
1. **Low effort, high pay** — best tier:
   - Mid-roll YouTube ad reads (just a segment in existing video)
   - X/Twitter sponsored tweet or thread
   - Quick product mention/shoutout in existing content
2. **Dedicated videos** — acceptable at higher price:
   - Full sponsored video about a product/service
   - Must be authentic to AJ's tech/AI niche
3. **Authenticity filter** — MUST pass:
   - Is this relevant to tech/AI/developer/creator audience?
   - Would AJ genuinely use or recommend this?
   - Does it align with "make tech cool" mission?
   - Is it a real company (not a scam/spam)?

## Label Assignment Rules

### Lead Scoring
- **Lead/Exceptional**: Collab offer >$2000, highly relevant to AJ's niche, from a known/reputable brand, low effort required
- **Lead/High**: Collab offer $500-$2000, relevant to tech/AI, legitimate brand, reasonable effort
- **Lead/Medium**: Collab offer $200-$500, somewhat relevant, or details unclear but promising
- **Lead/Low**: Collab offer <$200, or high effort for low pay, or tangentially relevant
- **Lead/Spam**: Fake/scam offers, mass-blast generic influencer spam, crypto scams, completely irrelevant products

### Stage (initial triage always starts as):
- **Stage/New**: Default for all new collab proposals

### Type Classification
- **Type/Talent-Agency**: From a talent/influencer agency or management company
- **Type/Platform-Notification**: Automated notifications from platforms (YouTube, GitHub, X, etc.)
- **Type/Tech-Update**: Tech newsletters, product updates, developer announcements

### Other Labels
- **Collab Proposal**: Any email that is a collaboration/sponsorship proposal
- **Collab Proposals <$500**: Subset where the offer is clearly under $500
- **Promotional**: Marketing/promotional emails that aren't collab proposals
- **Tech Updates**: Tech news, newsletters, product announcements

## Non-Collab Email Handling
- Platform notifications → `Type/Platform-Notification` + archive
- Tech newsletters/updates → `Type/Tech-Update` or `Tech Updates`
- Pure spam/marketing → `Promotional` + archive
- Personal/important emails → leave in inbox, no label (AJ handles manually)
- Augment Code work emails → leave in inbox, no label (AJ handles manually)

## Draft Reply Guidelines
For collab proposals rated Lead/Medium or above:
- Draft a reply in AJ's voice (casual, direct, friendly, tech-savvy)
- Express interest, ask for specifics if missing (budget, timeline, deliverables)
- For ad reads/tweets: "sounds interesting, what's the budget and timeline?"
- For dedicated videos: "I'm open to dedicated content at the right price point — what's the budget?"
- Never commit to anything in the draft — just open the conversation
- Keep it short (3-5 sentences max)
- Sign off as "AJ" or "Cheers, AJ"

## Execution Per Run
1. Fetch 10 most recent unread inbox emails without any user labels
2. Read each email thread
3. Classify and apply appropriate labels
4. For collab proposals Lead/Medium+, draft a reply
5. Mark as read after processing
6. Log summary of what was triaged

## Learning Over Time
- Agent will save memories about patterns (e.g., "Brand X is legit, offered $1500 before")
- AJ can provide feedback on triage decisions to refine criteria
- Rate thresholds may adjust as AJ's audience grows
