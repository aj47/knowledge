# Rounded Shoulders Correction Workout

**Goal:** Strengthen posterior chain (rear delts, rhomboids, lower traps) + stretch tight chest/anterior muscles.

---

## 🔥 Warm-up (5 min)
- Arm circles — 30 sec each direction
- Band pull-aparts — 3×15
- Cat-cow — 10 reps

---

## 💪 Strength (3 sets each)
| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Face Pulls | 15–20 | Cable or band, pull to forehead |
| Bent-over Dumbbell Rows | 10–12 | Squeeze shoulder blades |
| Rear Delt Flyes | 12–15 | Light weight, slow & controlled |
| Prone Y-T-W Raises | 10 each | On floor or bench, no weight needed |
| Serratus Wall Slides | 12–15 | Arms slide up wall, protract scapula |

---

## 🧘 Stretching (hold 30–60 sec each)
- **Doorway chest stretch** — critical for opening tight pecs
- **Pec minor stretch** — corner stretch, arms at 90°
- **Thread the needle** — thoracic rotation

---

## 📌 Key Tips
- **Face pulls are the #1 fix** — do them every session
- Avoid overdoing bench press / push-ups without balancing with rows
- Posture cue: think "proud chest, shoulders back and *down*"
- Do 3x/week — improvement in 4–6 weeks
