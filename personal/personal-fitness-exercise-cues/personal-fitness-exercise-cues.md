# Exercise Cues

## AJ split squat cues
- Front-leg biased stance
- Front foot fully planted: big toe, pinky toe base, heel
- Back leg is a kickstand, not the prime mover
- Slight forward torso lean to load quad + glute
- Hips and chest stay square
- Let the front knee travel forward over toes without collapse
- Lower for 3 seconds, own the bottom, drive up through full front foot
- Priorities: smooth knee path, no wobble, no crashing into the bottom

## Slow step-down cues
- Use a low box when available
- Foot tripod stays down
- Knee tracks over toes
- Hips stay level
- Slow 3-second lower, light tap, controlled return

## Reverse lunge cues
- Step back long enough to keep front foot flat
- Slight forward torso lean
- Front leg does the work
- Slow eccentric, stable knee path

## Spanish squat hold cues
- Band or strap behind knees anchored in front
- Lean back into support and sit down into a squat hold
- Feet flat, torso fairly upright
- Feel quads working hard without sharp joint pain

## Single-leg calf raise cues
- Big toe stays down
- Rise straight up, pause, lower slowly
- Avoid rolling side to side
- Use full range when available
