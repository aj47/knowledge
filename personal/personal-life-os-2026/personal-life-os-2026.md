# AJ Life OS
**Created:** 2026-03-13
**Purpose:** Turn AJ's goals, values, and work into a simple operating system that reduces drift and increases compounding.

---

Do fewer things, more consistently, with tighter feedback loops.

**What success looks like**

- Reliable core workflows
- Tight roadmap tied to real usage
- Visible user love and repeat use

**Primary metrics**
- Weekly product priority review completed
- Open PR/issue uncertainty reduced every week

**What success looks like**

**What success looks like**

- TechFren / AJ brand gains stronger recognition

**What success looks like**

- Tennis-prehab notes logged after workouts

## Life OS Architecture
This system runs on 5 layers.

### Layer 1: Direction
Used to prevent drift.
- Mission
- High-level goals
- Current quarter priorities
- Weekly focus

### Layer 2: Execution
Used to make progress visible.
- Daily top 1-3 priorities
- Project checkpoints
- Focus blocks
- Task capture + triage

### Layer 3: Review
Used to correct course.
- Daily shutdown
- Weekly review
- Monthly review
- Goal refresh

### Layer 4: Energy
Used to preserve output quality.
- Sleep / recovery awareness
- Fitness + knee durability
- Posture work
- Play / novelty / social energy

### Layer 5: Environment
Used to reduce friction.
- Clean notes structure
- Clear project dashboards
- Low-clutter workspace
- Reusable templates and repeat tasks

## The Core Systems

## 1. Daily Reset System
**Goal:** Start the day with clarity and end it without loose mental tabs.

### Morning (5-10 min)
Answer:
1. What matters most today?
2. What would make today a win?
3. What is the one thing I must not avoid?

Write:
- Today's top 3
- Main track: Augment or DotAgents
- Secondary support task
- Health intent (gym / tennis / walk / recovery)

### Shutdown (5 min)
Answer:
1. What moved forward today?
2. What is stuck?
3. What is the first step for tomorrow?

Log:
- Wins
- Open loops
- Tomorrow's first task

**Rule:** Never end the day with ambiguity about tomorrow's starting point.

---

## 2. Weekly Review System
**Goal:** Reconnect effort to goals and stop reactive drift.

### Weekly review checklist (30-45 min, Sunday or Monday)
- Review calendar and major outputs from the last 7 days
- Update progress on each high-level goal
- Identify what created momentum
- Identify what created drag
- Decide next week's top 3 outcomes
- Choose the one theme for the week
- Check health consistency
- Check personal/admin loose ends
- Review whether Augment and DotAgents both got intentional attention

### Weekly output questions
- What did I ship?
- What did I publish?
- What did I learn?
- What am I avoiding?
- What deserves more focus next week?

**Rule:** Weekly review produces a concrete next-week plan, not just reflection.

---

## 3. Monthly Reset System
**Goal:** Make sure life direction still matches effort.

### Monthly prompts
- Which of the 5 high-level goals gained real progress?
- Which one is drifting?
- What is the bottleneck right now: clarity, courage, skill, time, energy, or distribution?
- What should be stopped, automated, delegated, or simplified?
- What would make the next month feel meaningful?

### Monthly outputs
- Updated goal status
- 3 priorities for the month
- One thing to stop doing
- One system to improve

---

## 4. Product / Career Split System
**Goal:** Prevent DotAgents and Augment from cannibalizing each other.

### Rule set
- One deep work block = one track only
- Explicitly label blocks as `Augment`, `DotAgents`, `Content`, or `Life Admin`
- Don't mix product strategy with content production in the same block unless the output is directly connected
- If context feels fragmented, reduce commitments before increasing effort

### Minimum weekly standard
- Augment: 1 meaningful deliverable moved forward
- DotAgents: 1 meaningful product step moved forward
- Content: 1 publishable artifact completed

---

## 5. Content Compounding System
**Goal:** Turn thinking and building into discoverability.

### Weekly cadence target
- 1 substantial artifact: video, deep demo, or thread
- 1-2 shorter updates: progress note, clip, opinion, insight
- Repackage one idea across multiple channels when possible

### Content source hierarchy
1. Real product progress
2. Real opinions from hands-on work
3. Real lessons from Augment / AI tooling space
4. Real personal experiments and systems

**Rule:** Don't wait for inspiration. Document what is already happening.

---

## 6. Health / Durability System
**Goal:** Stay physically capable enough to support ambition.

### Non-negotiables
- 3-5 movement sessions/week
- Knee durability and tennis-prehab stay active
- Posture work remains part of routine
- Do not let more than 7 days pass without intentional training

### Minimum viable health week
- 2 strength sessions
- 1 Zone 2 session
- 1 mobility / prehab touchpoint
- 1 tennis session or lateral movement session

### Logging rule
After workouts, capture a quick note:
- What I did
- Pain / tightness / energy
- What to adjust next time

---

## 7. Scoreboard System
**Goal:** Measure what compounds.

Track weekly:
- Product shipped
- Content published
- Training sessions
- Number of meaningful deep work blocks
- Energy score (1-5)
- Drift score (1-5)

### Definitions
- **Energy score:** how physically and mentally capable AJ felt this week
- **Drift score:** how much time went to reactive or unfocused work
