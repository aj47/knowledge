# High-Level Goals and Systems — AJ
**Created:** 2026-03-13
**Purpose:** Define the bigger-picture goals shaping AJ's work and life, along with the operating systems that make them real.

---

## 1. Build DotAgents into a category-defining product

### Outcome
DotAgents becomes a credible, differentiated agent product with a clear identity, strong reliability, and visible user value.

### Success signals
- Product direction is clear and actively reflected in roadmap decisions
- Core user experience feels reliable enough to recommend without caveats
- Public artifacts regularly show progress: shipped features, demos, issue closure, releases
- Growth metrics trend upward: stars, installs, activation, retention, word of mouth

### System
- Weekly product review block to reorder priorities against real repo state
- Maintain a short, explicit "current priority sequence" instead of many parallel bets
- Close uncertainty fast: stale PRs, unclear issues, half-finished experiments
- Ship visible proof often: changelog items, demos, screenshots, short updates
- Treat roadmap clarity as a recurring practice, not a one-time planning task

### Cadence
- Weekly: roadmap refresh
- 2x/week: issue/PR triage
- Ongoing: publish visible progress when meaningful work lands

---

## 2. Become known for a strong point of view in AI agents and developer tools

### Outcome
AJ is not just building in the space, but becoming recognized as someone with taste, insight, and a clear voice on where AI agents are going.

### Success signals
- Consistent stream of posts, demos, clips, threads, and opinions
- People begin associating AJ with thoughtful work on agents, voice, workflows, and developer tooling
- Content compounds into reputation, inbound opportunities, and product discovery

### System
- Capture ideas during the week instead of waiting for inspiration
- Convert active work into content: what shipped, what failed, what changed, what was learned
- Keep a realistic cadence centered on high-signal updates, not constant posting pressure
- Reuse one insight across formats when possible: post, thread, short video, Discord recap, livestream talking point

### Cadence
- 3 high-signal public artifacts per week minimum
- 1 deeper opinion/demo piece per week when possible
- Maintain a running content backlog from current work

---

## 3. Run Augment and DotAgents as a sustainable two-track system

### Outcome
AJ performs strongly at Augment while still building DotAgents momentum, without constant context-switch debt or identity blur.

### Success signals
- Clear separation between Augment commitments and DotAgents priorities
- Each track has one concrete next deliverable at a time
- Less background guilt, less thrash, more deliberate focus
- Work in one domain sharpens the other rather than competing with it

### System
- Keep separate planning artifacts and success criteria for Augment vs DotAgents
- At all times, define the single most important next deliverable for each track
- Avoid mixing both into the same execution block unless there is a real leverage reason
- Use weekly planning to rebalance workload before drift becomes overload

### Cadence
- Weekly: choose one concrete Augment deliverable and one concrete DotAgents deliverable
- Daily: know which track owns the current work block

---

## 4. Increase distribution, not just output

### Outcome
More people discover, try, talk about, and share AJ's work.

### Success signals
- More GitHub stars, product awareness, social reach, and inbound conversations
- Public updates consistently point people toward a product, repo, demo, or clear next step
- Shipping translates into audience growth instead of staying invisible

### System
- Pair shipping with distribution by default
- Every meaningful product improvement should produce a public artifact or demo-worthy proof
- Reuse channels intentionally: X, Discord recaps, video snippets, repo updates, livestream mentions
- Track lightweight distribution metrics so momentum is visible

### Cadence
- For every meaningful ship: ask "what is the distribution artifact?"
- Weekly: review whether output is turning into reach

---

## 5. Build a personal operating system that compounds

### Outcome
AJ has a durable, low-chaos way of planning, reviewing, executing, and learning.

### Success signals
- Priorities stay current
- Notes are useful and findable
- Fewer stale plans and abandoned loops
- Regular reflection leads to better decisions and better systems

### System
- Keep a small number of canonical files for goals, priorities, and reviews
- Use short review loops to correct drift early
- Log important decisions and system changes so learning compounds
- Prefer simple recurring routines over complicated self-management frameworks

### Cadence
- Daily: quick priority reset
- Weekly: review goals, systems, and bottlenecks
- Monthly: remove stale commitments and rewrite the next operating focus

## 6. Stay physically durable, athletic, and energetic

### Outcome
Health supports ambition instead of lagging behind it.

### Success signals
- Consistent training with knee durability for tennis as a real priority
- Good baseline energy, posture, recovery, and mobility
- Workout notes are captured so progress is visible and adjustments are easy

### System
- Treat fitness as a performance enabler, not a side hobby
- Keep the longevity + posture + tennis-prehab base
- Log session notes immediately after workouts
- Prioritize consistency over novelty

### Cadence
- Weekly training rhythm maintained
- Post-workout notes logged same day
- Review pain, mobility, and performance trends weekly

## 7. Build a life that feels intentional and sustainable

### Outcome
AJ's work, health, environment, and personal life feel increasingly aligned rather than fragmented.

### Success signals
- Home routines support focus and recovery
- Pace feels ambitious but sustainable
- There is room for social energy, creativity, and reflection
- Personal life is designed, not deferred indefinitely

### System
- Protect recovery and margin
- Periodically evaluate whether current routines still match actual life
- Make decisions that reduce background friction in environment, schedule, and obligations
- Optimize for a life that can be maintained, not just survived

### Cadence
- Weekly check on energy, stress, and life admin
- Monthly reset on environment, routines, and personal alignment
