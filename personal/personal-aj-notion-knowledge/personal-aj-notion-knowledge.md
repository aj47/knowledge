## Mental Models & Life Principles
- Happiness is relative — don't trust your perception when you're up or down.
- Subconscious mind is far more powerful than conscious; it can be programmed (practice gratitude).
- ABE framework: Awareness → Beliefs → Excitement. Higher mind guides through excitement.
- Boredom is a signal you're safe — use it to pursue higher needs, not a problem to fix.
- The more immediate pleasure an action gives, the more you should question if it aligns with long-term goals.
- Craving surfing: delay a craving for 10 minutes — it weakens and you decide more rationally.
- Every thought is a cause, every condition an effect. Think as if you already are what you want to be.
- Great meditators aren't those who stay focused longer — they refocus faster.
- Meditate against your current state (inward vs outward distracted) to stay balanced.
- Environmental factors are a very strong influencer of behaviour.
- Old habits die young — work on 1% daily improvement.
