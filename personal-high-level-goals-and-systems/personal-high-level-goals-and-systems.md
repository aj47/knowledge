# AJ High-Level Goals & Systems
**Created:** 2026-03-13
**Purpose:** Turn AJ's current priorities into durable, high-level goals with operating systems that can be reviewed weekly and adjusted without losing direction.

---

## How to use this document
This is not a task list. It is the higher-level layer above tasks, issues, content ideas, and day-to-day execution.

Use it to answer three questions:
1. What kind of life and work am I building?
2. What systems make that inevitable?
3. What should I review every week so I don't drift?

---

## Goal 1: Build DotAgents into a category-defining product
### Outcome
DotAgents becomes a real product with clear positioning, strong reliability, obvious usefulness, and growing user pull.

### Why this matters
This is AJ's main personal project and strongest current startup opportunity. The goal is not just to ship features, but to create a product people remember, recommend, and return to.

### Core systems
- Maintain a single clear product priority order at all times.
- Run weekly roadmap review against actual GitHub issue/PR state.
- Prefer finishing and clarifying over opening new loops.
- Publish proof of usefulness regularly: demos, clips, examples, user wins.
- Keep the product identity sharp: DotAgents is its own thing, separate from Augment work.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Is there a current top 3 product priority list?
- Did at least one important product loop get fully closed this week?
- Did I reduce ambiguity in roadmap or PR state?
- Did I publish at least one public artifact showing progress or value?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't let closed issues continue to define current priorities.
- Don't confuse activity with product progress.
- Don't bury positioning under implementation details.

---

## Goal 2: Become known for AI agent + developer-tooling taste
### Outcome
AJ becomes recognized not just as someone who builds, but as someone with clear ideas, taste, and signal in AI agents, tooling, and the future of human-computer workflows.

### Why this matters
Reputation compounds. Strong public signal creates distribution, opportunities, trust, and leverage that pure building alone does not.

### Core systems
- Treat content as a strategic function, not leftover energy.
- Convert active work into public artifacts while momentum is high.
- Focus on high-signal content: demos, opinions, lessons, breakdowns, short updates.
- Reuse one idea across multiple formats when possible.
- Keep a running backlog of content angles generated from real work.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Did I publish something with signal this week?
- Did I turn at least one real work insight into content?
- Am I saying something memorable, not just documenting progress?
- Did I strengthen the association between AJ and this category?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't wait for perfect polish.
- Don't let content become disconnected from real building.
- Don't optimize for volume at the expense of signal.

---

## Goal 3: Build a sustainable two-track career system
### Outcome
AJ performs strongly at Augment while still compounding DotAgents and personal brand work without constant guilt, confusion, or context-switch chaos.

### Why this matters
This is the actual constraint environment. The goal is not to eliminate dual focus, but to design a system where both tracks benefit from AJ's strengths without cannibalizing each other.

### Core systems
- Keep Augment and DotAgents strategically separate.
- Define what each track is for:
  - Augment = career leverage, storytelling, technical content excellence, network.
  - DotAgents = ownership, experimentation, long-term upside, product identity.
- Capture crossovers carefully, but avoid brand confusion.
- Plan weekly around energy and context, not just urgency.
- Ensure each week includes one meaningful win in each track.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Did Augment move forward in a concrete way?
- Did DotAgents move forward in a concrete way?
- Did I keep the two narratives clear?
- Did I avoid letting one track fully erase the other?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't mix brands carelessly.
- Don't let reactive work consume all creator/builder energy.
- Don't judge balance by feelings alone; judge by visible outputs.

## Goal 4: Build a stronger personal engine
### Outcome
AJ develops a personal operating system that makes consistent execution, reflection, and self-correction normal instead of heroic.

### Why this matters
A better system beats mood-dependent effort. This is the layer that supports every other goal.

### Core systems
- Keep a current goals document and revisit it weekly.
- Review open loops, priorities, and unfinished decisions on a set cadence.
- Use simple scoreboards instead of vague self-judgment.
- Write things down when emotionally tangled or mentally noisy.
- Aim for 1% improvement and reduced drift, not dramatic resets.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Did I review goals and reset priorities?
- Did I reduce open loops instead of multiplying them?
- Did I document decisions that were previously fuzzy?
- Did I spend most of my time on the right category of work?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't rely on memory for priorities.
- Don't wait until overwhelmed to review.
- Don't confuse introspection with progress unless it changes behavior.

## Goal 5: Stay physically durable, energetic, and attractive to life
### Outcome
AJ maintains the health, movement quality, posture, and energy needed to support a long, ambitious, creative life.

### Why this matters
Health is not separate from ambition. It is the foundation that makes output, confidence, mood, and longevity possible.

### Core systems
- Keep fitness focused on durability, longevity, and tennis readiness.
- Log workout notes immediately after sessions.
- Prioritize knee resilience, posture correction, grip/general strength, and consistency over intensity theater.
- Keep exercise regular enough to compound without becoming unrealistic.
- Treat recovery and physical maintenance as part of performance, not a side quest.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Did I complete the planned training sessions?
- Did I log notes after workouts?
- Did I maintain knee/prehab work?
- Do I feel physically more prepared for tennis and daily life?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't let all exercise become optional when work gets intense.
- Don't optimize for short bursts of motivation.
- Don't separate aesthetics, performance, and longevity too much; build a durable base.

## Goal 6: Build a stable, meaningful adult life
### Outcome
AJ builds a life that feels grounded, generous, and expansive: strong marriage, stable home, financial progress, future family readiness, and enough freedom to enjoy what is being built.

### Why this matters
Success is not just product momentum. The long game includes love, home, money, family, peace of mind, and the ability to live well.

### Core systems
- Protect relationship quality with Chloe through time, presence, and communication.
- Build toward financial calm, not just revenue ambition.
- Keep the long-term targets visible: house, family, comfortable travel, worry-free living.
- Make life decisions that support stability as well as upside.
- Avoid building a career that wins publicly but feels hollow privately.

### Weekly scoreboard
- Did I invest in my relationship this week?
- Did I make at least one decision that improved long-term stability?
- Am I behaving in a way that aligns with future family and home goals?
- Is the way I am working compatible with the life I want later?

### Anti-drift rules
- Don't postpone life indefinitely for momentum.
- Don't chase public progress while neglecting private foundations.
- Don't forget that freedom includes emotional and financial safety.

## Master operating principle
The point is not to do everything at once.
The point is to keep all six domains alive while choosing one or two to emphasize each week.

A good week is not perfect balance.
A good week is one where the important things continue to compound.

---

## Weekly review template
Use this once per week:

### 1. Which goal needs the most attention right now?
Choose 1 primary domain and 1 secondary domain.

### 2. What would visible progress look like this week?
Define one concrete win for the primary domain.
Define one concrete win for the secondary domain.

### 3. Where am I drifting?
Look for:
- unclear priorities
- too many open loops
- too little publishing
- too much reactive work
- neglected health
- neglected relationship or life admin

### 4. What are this week's non-negotiables?
Keep this short.

### 5. What should I stop doing?
Name one behavior creating drag, confusion, or fragmentation.
