# AJ Strategy — Rest of 2026

**Created:** 2026-03-29
**Anchored to:** July 2026 inflection point (visa constraints lift, need ~$60k/yr independent income)

---

## The Honest Assessment

You're 3 months from the inflection point. The core tension hasn't changed: you're building something ambitious (DotAgents as category-defining product + open standard) while needing to generate real income by summer. You can't do 5 things at 20% each. You need to do 2 things at high intensity.

**The two things that matter:**
1. **Ship DotAgents to the point it generates revenue or undeniable traction**
2. **Build audience large enough that it creates opportunities (content, sponsorships, consulting, or product distribution)**

Everything else is support structure.

---

## PHASE 1: SPRINT TO PROOF (April – May 2026)

**Theme:** Make DotAgents undeniably real and the audience visibly growing.

### Product (60% of time)
- **Lock the brand.** Finalize naming, domain, visual identity. Stop the namespace confusion. Ship the decision by April 7 and move on.
- **Ship one killer demo.** Not 10 half-features — one workflow that makes someone say "I need this." Voice-first agent that does something no other tool does cleanly.
- **Get to stable release.** Merge backlog PRs, fix the reliability issues that prevent you from recommending it without caveats. Target a version you'd put in front of a stranger.
- **Define the monetization hook.** What is someone paying for? Pro features? Hosted agents? Skill marketplace? Pick ONE and build just enough to validate it.
- **Hub starter packs.** Ship 3-5 curated .agents/ starter packs that show the open standard's value.

### Audience (40% of time)
- **3 tweets/day minimum.** Build-in-public, hot takes on AI agents, short demos. Use the tweet optimizer skill.
- **1 YouTube video every 2 weeks.** Focus on "I built X with AI agents" format — showcases product AND teaches.
- **Discord engagement.** Weekly recap posts, respond to community. This is your early-adopter base.
- **Target: 5K X followers, 1K YouTube subs by end of May.**

### Weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: Plan the week (1 product goal, 1 content piece, 1 community action)
- Tue-Thu: Deep product work mornings, content afternoons
- Friday: Ship something visible (release, demo, video, thread)
- Weekend: Light content + recharge

### Phase 1 Exit Criteria
- [ ] Brand finalized and shipped
- [ ] One demo that gets organic sharing
- [ ] Stable release people can install and use
- [ ] Monetization hook identified and prototyped
- [ ] Audience growth trend established (not vanity — engagement rate)

---

## PHASE 2: LOCK REVENUE PATH (June – July 2026)

**Theme:** Convert proof into money or fundable traction before the inflection point.

### Revenue tracks (pick the one with signal from Phase 1)

**Track A: Product revenue**
- Launch paid tier (Pro/Teams). Even $10/mo proves the model.
- Target: 50 paying users by end of July = $6k ARR signal
- This won't hit $60k alone, but proves the path

**Track B: Content + consulting revenue**
- If audience is growing, monetize through: sponsored content, AI agent consulting, paid workshops
- Target: 2-3 paid engagements or sponsorships
- This can bridge income while product scales

**Track C: Hybrid (most likely)**
- Product generates some revenue + audience creates consulting/sponsorship income
- The audience IS the distribution channel for the product
- Target: $2-5k/month combined by end of July

### Product focus
- Ship the paid features from Phase 1 prototype
- Onboarding flow that converts — someone goes from download to "wow" in under 5 minutes
- Public roadmap that shows momentum (GitHub, changelog, social)

### Audience focus
- Increase to 1 YouTube video/week if the format is working
- Start an email list / newsletter if X engagement converts
- Guest appearances on AI podcasts — leverage the "solo builder shipping real agent product" story
- **Target: 10K X followers, 3K YouTube subs by end of July**

### Phase 2 Exit Criteria
- [ ] Revenue is real — at least $1-2k/month from some combination
- [ ] Clear answer to "what's the path to $5k/month?"
- [ ] Visa decision made with confidence (stay indie vs. take a role vs. hybrid)

---

## PHASE 3: SCALE OR PIVOT (August – December 2026)

**Theme:** Double down on what's working, cut what isn't.

### If product revenue is working
- Hire or contract help for the non-core work (design, docs, community management)
- Ship enterprise/team features
- Target: $5k/month by October, $8-10k/month by December
- Raise a small round if traction justifies it (angel/pre-seed)

### If audience/consulting is working
- Productize the consulting (templates, courses, paid community)
- DotAgents becomes the credibility engine — the thing that proves you know agents
- Target: $5k/month from content + consulting by October

### If neither is working by August
- Honest assessment time. Options:
  - Take a role at an AI company (your skills are extremely marketable) while continuing DotAgents nights/weekends
  - Pivot DotAgents to a narrower, more sellable wedge
  - Double down on content as the primary income path with product as portfolio
- This isn't failure — it's information. The July inflection point exists specifically so you can make this call with data.

### Regardless of path
- **Keep shipping publicly.** Whatever you're doing, the audience sees progress.
- **Keep the .agents/ standard alive.** Even if the desktop app pivots, the open standard can have a life of its own.
- **Health stays non-negotiable.** 3-5 workouts/week, weekly reset. You can't sprint for 9 months on empty.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Watch

1. **Parallel bet creep.** You've already identified this. If you catch yourself with 4+ active workstreams, something needs to die.
2. **Perfection before shipping.** The brand doesn't need to be perfect. The app doesn't need to be perfect. Ship, get feedback, iterate.
3. **Content without product progress.** Tweeting about building is not building. Content should showcase real shipped work.
4. **Isolation.** You're solo. Actively seek feedback, co-creation, and accountability. Discord community, Twitter mutuals, IRL meetups.
5. **Ignoring revenue signals.** If someone offers to pay for something, that's the most important data point you'll get. Follow it.

---

## Monthly Check-in Questions

Ask yourself on the 1st of each month:
1. What did I ship last month that someone else can use?
2. Is my audience growing? What's driving it?
3. Am I closer to $60k/year independent income? By how much?
4. What should I stop doing?
5. Am I healthy and sustainable, or burning out?

---

## The Bottom Line

July is not a cliff — it's a checkpoint. The goal isn't to have everything figured out by then. The goal is to have **enough signal** to make a confident decision about your next move. That signal comes from shipping real things, putting them in front of real people, and seeing what sticks.

The worst outcome isn't that DotAgents doesn't become a billion-dollar company. The worst outcome is arriving at July with nothing shipped, no audience, and no data — still deliberating instead of having tried.

Ship fast. Ship ugly. Ship often. The rest follows.
