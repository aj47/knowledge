# Brand Deals Strategy

## Objective
Prepare now for June monetization so outreach is strategic, trust-preserving, and aligned with AJ's audience.

## Positioning
AJ should be positioned as a trusted evaluator and builder-facing operator, not just a promoter.

## Ideal partner profile
- Long-term relationship preferred over one-off sponsorships
- Product is genuinely useful to AI builders
- Clear technical differentiation
- Audience fit is strong
- Product can appear naturally in workflows, comparisons, or build content

## Example target tiers
### Tier 1
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- xAI
- Perplexity
- Cursor

### Tier 2
- Replit
- other infrastructure or agent-tooling companies with real technical differentiation

### Tier 3
- opportunistic but non-core partnerships to evaluate carefully

## Evaluation criteria
- real audience value
- technical differentiation
- credibility risk / conflict-of-interest risk
- repeat integration potential
- revenue potential
- strategic upside beyond one video

## Deal structure preferences
1. long-term partnership
2. recurring integration series
3. comparison-based sponsorships with editorial trust preserved
4. selective one-offs only when strategically useful

## Immediate outputs needed
- target brand list
- pricing / packaging draft
- outreach templates
- CRM tracking entries
- weekly pipeline review
