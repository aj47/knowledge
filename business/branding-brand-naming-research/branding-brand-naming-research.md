# DotAgents Rebrand (formerly SpeakMCP) - Naming & Domain Research
> **NOTE (2026-03):** SpeakMCP is now deprecated. The project is DotAgents (aj47/dotagents-mono). This file is kept for historical reference.

Date: 2025-06-10

## Concept
Pivot from "SpeakMCP" (command center framing) to a **community/society of AI agents** — each with a personality and specialty, all accessible by voice. Ties into @techfren / @techfren_ai brand.

Core pitch: "A community of AI frens — each one a specialist, all of them yours. Just talk."

Logo note: Current logo looks like an S — nice to keep if possible.

## Name Ideas & Analysis

### S-shaped logo compatible
- **Synth** — taken
- **Sovr** — available! Short, premium feel, 4 letters. No fren connection.
- **Swarm** — taken
- **Sphera** — taken
- **Speakfrens** — available! On-brand, hints at voice interface + frens community

### Frenz variants (with Z)
- frenz.com — taken
- frenzio.com — available, clean/punchy, startup energy
- joinfrenz.com — available, great CTA
- talktofrenz.com — available
- frenzchat.com — available

### Fren variants (no Z)
- **FrenCore** — available, technical/founder feel
- **FrenNet** — taken
- **FrenCrew** — taken
- **FrenSociety** — available, captures "society of agents" concept well

---

## Top Recommendations
1. **speakfrens.com** — most on-brand, voice + frens + community
2. **frensociety.com** — best captures "society of AI agents" concept
3. **frenzio.com** — short, punchy, startup feel
4. **sovr.com** — most premium/clean if going direction-agnostic
