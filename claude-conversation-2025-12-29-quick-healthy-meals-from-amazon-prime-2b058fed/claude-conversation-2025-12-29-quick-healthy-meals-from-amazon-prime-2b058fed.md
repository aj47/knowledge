**Basic ratio:** 1:1 oats to oat milk (e.g., ½ cup each), plus a handful of frozen berries on top.

**Tips for best results:**
- Add the frozen berries on top rather than mixed in — they'll thaw overnight and create a nice syrupy layer
- Oat milk is thinner than dairy, so you might want slightly less liquid (start with equal parts and adjust next time)
- Add chia seeds (1 tbsp) if you want a thicker, more pudding-like texture
- A splash of vanilla or drizzle of honey rounds it out
