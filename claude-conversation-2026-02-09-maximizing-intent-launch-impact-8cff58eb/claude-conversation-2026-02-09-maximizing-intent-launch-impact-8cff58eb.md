**What Codex App is missing (vs Intent):**
- **No spec-driven development** — This is the biggest gap. Codex agents work from prompts, not from living specifications. There's no equivalent to Intent's self-maintaining specs. Plan mode is close but it's per-task, not a persistent project-level spec.

- **OpenAI models only** — No BYOA. You use GPT-5.3-Codex or GPT-5.1-Codex-Mini. Can't bring Claude, local models, or anything else.
- **Cloud execution model** — During cloud tasks, internet access is disabled. The agent works in isolation. This is secure but limiting for tasks that need external APIs or services.
